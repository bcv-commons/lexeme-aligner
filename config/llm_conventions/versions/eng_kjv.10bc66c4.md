Target positions are the letter runs of the verse in reading order. Punctuation is dropped; a possessive `'s` attaches to the
word before it. Position numbers refer to that tokenization.

English (King James Version, 1769 text). The source is the Textus Receptus (NT) or the Hebrew Masoretic text (OT). Rules for
function words — each goes to ITS OWN source word when the source has one; only when it has none does it join the span of the
content word whose grammar it expresses:

- **Articles `the` / `a` / `an`**: `the` renders the Greek article ὁ / Hebrew ה when there is one; otherwise it belongs to the
  noun's span. `a` / `an` have no source word: they belong to the noun's span.
- **`of`**: a genitive with no preposition of its own: `of` belongs to the genitive noun's span (`of David`).
- **Possessives `his` / `their` / `my` / `thy` / `your`**: usually a separate Greek pronoun (αὐτοῦ, αὐτῶν, μου, σου, ὑμῶν) or a
  Hebrew suffix: link to that, NOT to the article before the noun.
- **Auxiliaries `shall` / `will` / `hath` / `was` / `be` / `is` / `are` / `had` / `do`**: carried by the verb's ending; they belong to
  the verb's span. The infinitive `to` belongs to the verb's span too.
- **Subject pronouns** (`he`, `I`, `they`, `thou`, `ye`): a separate Greek pronoun (αὐτός, ἐγώ, σύ, ὑμεῖς) when there is one;
  otherwise carried by the verb: the verb's span.
- **Archaic forms are ordinary renderings:** `thou` / `thee` / `thy` / `thine` / `ye` render σύ / ὑμεῖς and their cases; `-est` /
  `-eth` endings are part of one word (`knoweth`). `unto` is a preposition (πρός, εἰς, or the dative). `verily` renders ἀμήν,
  `behold` renders ἰδού.
- **Words in italics in the KJV** were supplied by the translators (no word behind them, e.g. `are` in "from Abraham to David *are*
  fourteen generations"). Give a supplied word to the source word it completes when it belongs to that word's rendering (a copula,
  an article, a pronoun the construction needs); otherwise it renders nothing.
