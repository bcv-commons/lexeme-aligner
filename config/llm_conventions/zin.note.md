Request format (lexeme-aligner): `translation_text` is the text as written (Latin script, tone marks kept). The positions of
`translation_words` are the PARTS of the words: a word the analysis divides appears as several translation words, each with
`in_word` (the written word it belongs to) and `part` (prefix, stem or suffix); a word that is not divided has neither.
`akaba naayolekeelela` (MRK 1:7) is two written words and seven translation words: `a` `ka` (prefixes) `ba` (stem) of `akaba`,
then `na` `a` (prefixes) `yolekeelel` (stem) `a` (suffix) of `naayolekeelela`. Answer about the parts; a part marked `prefix`
with the same letters as a free word (`na`, `ni`, `ba`, `ti`) is the prefix, not that word.

Free words, from lexeme-aligner's statistical alignment of this edition (tendencies, not rules; check each in its clause):
- `na` / `no` / `ne` render καί (and "with" = μετά / σύν); `náho` renders δέ; `omu` / `omuli` render ἐν / εἰς and with `kuluga`
  ἐκ / ἀπό; `aha` / `ha` ἐπί or a location; `hali` / `ahali` πρός; `kuba` / `habwo` γάρ or ὅτι ("because"); `nikwo` / `nka` ὅτι or
  ὡς; `mbwéenu` / `bwéecho` οὖν; `aáti` introduces speech (λέγων / ὅτι).
- Pronouns: `íinye` ἐγώ, `íichwe` ἡμεῖς, `íiwe` σύ, `íimwe` ὑμεῖς; possessives follow the noun (`waanze` "my", `waawe` "your",
  `boómwe` "your" pl.).
- `Múungu` = θεός.
